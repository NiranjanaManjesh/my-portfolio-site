Annotated TAF vFinal

This is the final annotated version of a standard Terminal Aerodrome Forecast (TAF). The example below shows the forecast groups and their meaning.

Final TAF Example

TAF KJFK 121130Z 1212/1318 23012KT P6SM FEW020 SCT050
TEMPO 1215/1218 4SM -SHRA BR BKN015 OVC030
FM130000 20015G25KT P6SM SCT020 BKN040

Annotation Guide

TAF — forecast type: Terminal Aerodrome Forecast.
KJFK — ICAO station identifier for John F. Kennedy International Airport.
121130Z — issuance time: 12th day at 1130 UTC.
1212/1318 — valid period: from 12th 1200 UTC through 13th 1800 UTC.
23012KT — wind: 230 degrees at 12 knots.
P6SM — visibility: greater than 6 statute miles.
FEW020 SCT050 — sky condition: few clouds at 2,000 feet, scattered clouds at 5,000 feet.

Temporary Conditions

TEMPO 1215/1218 — temporary conditions expected from 121500 UTC to 121800 UTC.
4SM — temporary visibility 4 statute miles.
-SHRA — light rain showers.
BR — mist.
BKN015 OVC030 — broken clouds at 1,500 feet, overcast at 3,000 feet.

From-Time Change

FM130000 — new forecast begins at 130000 UTC.
20015G25KT — wind changes to 200 degrees at 15 knots gusting 25 knots.
P6SM — visibility remains greater than 6 statute miles.
SCT020 BKN040 — sky condition: scattered clouds at 2,000 feet, broken clouds at 4,000 feet.

How to Read This Final Version

This annotated TAF includes the final forecast structure and highlights the standard groups used in TAF decoding. The order of groups in a typical TAF is:

  • TAF header
  • station identifier
  • issuance time
  • valid period
  • winds, visibility, sky condition, and weather
  • any TEMPO, PROB, FM, or BECMG amendments
